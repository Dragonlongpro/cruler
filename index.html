CRuler 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-10-26 适用：CRuler（iOS）

一、总则

CRuler 是一款屏幕测量工具，用于把手机屏幕当作尺子测量小物件尺寸。

我们不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人信息。App 无需注册或登录即可使用全部功能。

界面语言：跟随系统设置自动切换简体中文、香港繁体中文或英文，App 内不提供语言开关； 你也可以在 iOS 设置中为 CRuler 单独指定语言。语言选择本身不涉及任何个人数据，也不会写入 App 的本地存储。

本政策适用于 CRuler iOS 1.0 及后续版本。

二、我们不收集的信息

CRuler 不收集以下任何信息：

  • 姓名、手机号、邮箱、地址等身份信息
  • 设备标识符（IDFA、IDFV）、广告标识
  • 位置信息
  • 通讯录、短信、通话记录
  • 使用行为、崩溃日志、分析统计数据
  • 相册内容

App 内不含任何第三方 SDK（无广告、无统计分析、无社交登录、无崩溃上报）， 也不进行跨 App 或跨网站追踪，不使用 ATT（App Tracking Transparency）。

隐私清单：App 内置 PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy，明确声明不追踪、不收集任何数据类型； 仅因保存本地偏好设置而访问 UserDefaults（Apple 原因代码 CA92.1）。

三、仅保存在你设备本地的数据

以下数据保存在 App 的本地存储（UserDefaults 与 App 容器内的 Application Support）中， 不会上传到我们的服务器：

数据用途
主题、配色与弥散光斑布局记住你选的外观
弥散强度、标尺颜色同步、「跟随滑杆滚动刻度」记住显示与操作偏好
触感反馈开关、拟物音效开关与音色记住触感 / 音效偏好
机型校准结果（机型型号、每毫米像素数、长边尺寸）保证测量精度
自定义背景图片与元数据（最多 6 个槽位、每日随机历史、派生配色）作为测量界面的背景
首次启动标记判断是否完成首次引导

机型校准会读取设备型号字符串（如 iPhone17,2）、屏幕尺寸与像素密度， 仅在本机用于换算；不读取 IDFA / IDFV，也不会上传。

标记位置、锁定状态与工具栏显隐仅在本次运行内有效，不写入本地存储。 界面语言同样不落盘，只读取系统语言设置。

删除 App 会清除 App 容器内的上述本地数据。iOS 可能按照你的备份设置， 将 App 数据一并纳入 iCloud 或电脑备份；该备份由系统与你管理，开发者无法访问。

四、相册访问说明

App 的「自定义背景」功能使用 iOS 系统提供的 PhotosPicker 选择图片：

  • 由系统在独立进程中呈现选择界面，App 不会获得整个相册的访问权限
  • 只有你主动选中的那一张图片会被读取，并在设备本地裁剪、压缩后存入 App 容器 （最多 6 个自定义背景槽位）
  • 「每天随机显示图片」只从你已保存在 App 本地的这些图片中选择，不读取相册其他内容
  • 图片不会上传到任何服务器
  • 你不使用该功能时，App 不会访问相册

五、网络与权限

  • CRuler 不需要联网即可正常使用；App 自身不发起网络请求，也不含第三方网络 SDK
  • 点击「设置 → 关于 → 隐私政策」会调用系统浏览器打开本页面； 浏览器可能访问网络，其行为受浏览器与 iOS 的隐私政策约束；App 不嵌入 WebView，也不读取浏览内容
  • 本页面由 GitHub Pages 托管；访问本页面时，GitHub 可能按其隐私政策记录标准服务器日志 （如 IP 地址、浏览器信息）。该日志由 GitHub 管理，与 App 无关
  • 不申请定位、麦克风、相机、通讯录、通知等权限；音效仅用于播放，不录音、不使用麦克风
  • 不进行跨 App 或跨网站追踪，不使用 IDFA / IDFV 或 ATT

六、儿童隐私

App 不收集任何个人信息，因此也不存在针对儿童的专门数据收集行为。

七、你的权利

由于我们不收集任何个人数据，也不存在需要导出、更正或删除的服务器端数据。

如需清除全部本地数据，请在 iOS 设置中删除本 App，或使用 App 内的重置功能。 如你开启了 iCloud / 电脑备份，历史备份中的 App 数据可能仍由系统保留，需在备份管理中处理。

八、政策变更

若本政策发生变更，我们会在本页面更新「生效日期」，并在重要变更时通过 App 更新说明告知。

九、本政策在 App 内的访问方式

你可以在 App 内「设置 → 关于 → 隐私政策」随时打开本政策页面。

十、联系我们

如对本政策或隐私保护有任何疑问，请通过 App Store 应用详情页中提供的开发者联系方式与我们联系。

1. Overview

CRuler is a screen measuring tool that turns your phone's screen into a ruler for measuring small objects.

We do not collect, upload, or share any personal information. Every feature is available without registration or sign-in.

Language: the app follows your system language and switches automatically between Simplified Chinese, Hong Kong Traditional Chinese, and English. There is no in-app language switch; you may also set a language for CRuler in iOS Settings. Your language choice involves no personal data and is not written to the app's local storage.

This policy applies to CRuler for iOS 1.0 and later versions.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

CRuler does not collect any of the following:

  • Identity information such as name, phone number, email address, or postal address
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV) or advertising identifiers
  • Location information
  • Contacts, messages, or call history
  • Usage behavior, crash logs, or analytics data
  • Photo library contents

The app contains no third-party SDKs (no advertising, analytics, social login, or crash reporting), and it does not track you across apps or websites. It does not use ATT (App Tracking Transparency).

Privacy manifest: the app bundles PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy, which declares no tracking and no collected data types. The only accessed-API declaration is UserDefaults (Apple reason CA92.1), used solely to remember local preferences.

3. Data Stored Only on Your Device

The following data is stored in the app's local storage (UserDefaults and Application Support inside the app container) and is never uploaded to our servers:

DataPurpose
Theme, colors, and diffuse-blob layoutRemember your appearance choice
Diffuse intensity, ruler color sync, and "Scroll with slider"Remember display and interaction preferences
Haptics toggle, sound toggle, and sound choiceRemember haptic and sound preferences
Device calibration results (model, pixels per millimeter, long-edge size)Ensure measurement accuracy
Custom background images and metadata (up to 6 slots, daily-random history, derived palette)Serve as the measuring interface background
First-launch flagDetermine whether onboarding is complete

Device calibration reads the device model string (for example iPhone17,2), screen size, and pixel density, and uses them only on-device for conversion. It does not read IDFA / IDFV and never uploads them.

Marker positions, lock state, and toolbar visibility are valid only for the current session and are not written to local storage. The interface language is not stored either; only the system language setting is read.

Deleting the app removes this local data from the app container. iOS may include app data in your iCloud or computer backups according to your backup settings; those backups are managed by the system and by you, and the developer cannot access them.

4. About Photo Library Access

The "Custom Background" feature uses the system PhotosPicker provided by iOS to select images:

  • The picker is presented by the system in a separate process; the app never receives access to your entire photo library
  • Only the single image you explicitly select is read, then cropped and compressed locally on your device and stored in the app container (up to 6 custom background slots)
  • "Randomize daily" only chooses among images already stored locally in the app; it does not read other photo library content
  • Images are never uploaded to any server
  • If you do not use this feature, the app does not access your photo library

5. Network and Permissions

  • CRuler works fully offline; the app itself makes no network requests and contains no third-party network SDKs
  • Tapping "Settings → About → Privacy Policy" opens this page in the system browser. The browser may access the network under its own and iOS privacy terms; the app does not embed a WebView or read your browsing content
  • This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may record standard server logs (such as IP address and browser information) under its own privacy policy. Those logs are managed by GitHub, not by the app
  • It does not request location, microphone, camera, contacts, or notification permissions. Sound is played back only; the app does not record audio or use the microphone
  • It does not track you across apps or websites and does not use IDFA / IDFV or ATT

6. Children's Privacy

The app collects no personal information, so there is no child-specific data collection.

7. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is no server-side data to export, correct, or delete.

To erase all local data, delete the app in iOS Settings or use the reset function inside the app. If you have iCloud or computer backups enabled, app data in historical backups may still be retained by the system; manage that through your backup settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Effective date" on this page and notify you through the app's release notes for material changes.

9. Accessing This Policy in the App

You can open this policy at any time in the app via Settings → About → Privacy Policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or about privacy protection, please contact us using the developer contact information provided on the App Store product page.
